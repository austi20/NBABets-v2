## 4. How much should the model be trusted against the anchor?

Least squares through the origin on `(actual - anchor) = w * (model - anchor)`, anchor = the project's `history_anchor`. w = 1 means the stage is already weighted right, w < 1 means it should be pulled toward the anchor (over trusted), w > 1 means it is under trusted. The leave one season out columns show how much w moves; a coefficient of variation above 25% is noise.
